# 2026-11-15: silent calls to G.723 phones that also offer DTMF (Allen Computer Repair)

The first **large** customer, Allen Computer Repair (98 extensions, 3 numbers), signed up at
8:56 PM ET on 2026-11-14. Within 20 minutes the course counted failed **Extension → extension** and
**Desk phones ringing** legs (buckets 9:10–9:20 PM), and our new `CustomerCallAudioBad` alert fired
on edge-1 (`silent_to_party`, 02:11 UTC). Times below are UTC unless marked ET.

## What the audio record showed

`/var/log/pbx/call-audio.jsonl` (pbx-call-audio, PR #58) on the edges, customer legs since 01:50:
57 legs, 3 not ok, all `silent_to_party`, all ~7 s, all toward phones at 130.245.136.125 using
**G.723** (2) or **G.729** (1). On every one, rtpengine forwarded the phone's audio to Asterisk
(~250 packets) and received **0 packets from Asterisk**. The G.729 leg was the caller of the
02:11 extension → extension call whose callee was a G.723 phone: both heard nothing.

## Cause

Asterisk (pbx-2) at the moment extension 193 answered (02:10:30):

```
NOTICE res_pjsip_sdp_rtp.c: No joint capabilities for 'audio' media stream between our
configuration((ulaw)) and incoming SDP((nothing))
```

The edge-1 capture shows why. The phone answered G.723 with an `fmtp` line plus DTMF; rtpengine
rewrote that answer for Asterisk with **no audio codec at all**:

| | SDP |
|---|---|
| Phone's 200 OK | `m=audio … RTP/AVP 4 101`, `a=fmtp:4 annexa=no`, `a=rtpmap:101 telephone-event/8000` |
| Forwarded to Asterisk | `m=audio … RTP/AVP 101` (telephone-event only) |

Asterisk then sent nothing, and rtpengine sent the G.723 phone PCMU it can't play.

Every phone answer in the edge captures, paired with what reached Asterisk: PCMU, PCMA, G.729
(with or without DTMF), G.723 alone and Opus all came out as PCMU; the only broken combination was
**G.723 + telephone-event**, 3 of 3. The codec quality test (PR #53/#57) never hit it because its
G.723 phone answered without DTMF.

## Reproduction (rtpengine 26.0.1.28)

Offer/answer replayed directly on rtpengine's control port (edge-2, throwaway call ids), then real
RTP between two sockets on the edge (nothing sent to real parties):

| Phone's answer | Live flags | + `reuse-codecs` on the answer |
|---|---|---|
| `4 101` + `a=fmtp:4 annexa=no` (also `annexa=yes`, `bitrate=6.3`) | to Asterisk `101`; phone gets PCMU | to Asterisk `0 101`; phone gets G.723 (24-byte frames every 30 ms); DTMF from the phone reaches Asterisk |
| `4 101` without fmtp | `0 101`, works | same |
| `4` + fmtp, no DTMF | `0`, works | same |
| `0/8/18 (+101)` | works | identical |
| `96 101` (Opus) | `0 101` | `0`: **drops DTMF toward Asterisk**, so not used for Opus |
| `4 0 8 18 101` | phone gets G.723 | phone gets PCMU (it listed it too) |

Then end to end from mon-1 (02:38:24): test phone B answering `4 101` + `annexa=no` reproduced the
same Asterisk notice and the same `m=audio … 101` toward Asterisk.

Tried and not working: G.723 fmtp in `codec-transcode=` (rtpengine ignores it), `always-transcode`,
`single-codec`, `codec-accept`, `codec-consume`, `codec-except`, `allow-transcoding`,
`codec-transcode=PCMU` on the answer.

## Fix

Kamailio `route[G723_FIX]`: when a phone's answer contains `a=fmtp:4 ` (a G.723 parameter line), add
`reuse-codecs` to that `rtpengine_answer` (ringing branches in `DEV_REPLY`, in-dialog answers in
`RTP_REPLY`). Every other answer is processed exactly as before. `kamailio -c` passes on edge-1.

The codec quality test's phones now include `telephone-event` (pt 101) in their offers and answers,
like the course's phones, so its G.723 callee case exercises this path.

## Verify after deploying

- Rerun the mon-1 reproduction (`UAS=t_uas_g723.xml bash /root/repro/run2.sh 130.245.136.155 0`):
  the 200 OK forwarded to Asterisk should read `m=audio … 0 101`, and Asterisk should log no
  "No joint capabilities".
- `pbx_quality_audio_ok{codec="G723"}` stays 1 with the new DTMF-carrying test phones.
- No new `silent_to_party` customer legs toward G.723 phones in `call-audio.jsonl`.

## Also checked: REGISTER p95 ~550 ms on the course chart

Not our processing. From edge-1's capture, over ~6,700 UDP registrations: a whole registration
(first REGISTER in → 200 out) took p50 4.8 ms, p95 14 ms; about 1 % took ~507 ms, i.e. the phone's
500 ms retransmission after its packet never reached us. That is the platform's packet loss on
the client → edge path (README §3.2 NET-1), which our timers can't shorten.
